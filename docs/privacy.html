Privacy policy · effective 15 November 2026

What you receive stays on your phone.

This policy covers the Android app Messages Recovery (package com.messagesrecovery.app), published by Mulhimfy.

In short

  • Messages Recovery collects no personal data and shares nothing with anyone.
  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything off your phone.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything the app keeps is stored only on your phone, and you can erase it at any time.

Notification access

To do its job, the app asks you to turn on Android's notification access. You turn it on yourself in Android's settings, and you can turn it off there at any time.

With that access, the app reads the notifications of the apps you choose (chat and messaging apps are selected at first; you can change the list in the app). From those notifications it keeps, on your phone only:

  • the name of the chat and of the sender, and the sender's small picture when the notification carries one;
  • the text of each message and the time it was sent;
  • a picture attached to the notification, when there is one.

The app uses this only to show you those messages inside the app, and to mark a message when its sender later deletes or edits it. Notifications of apps you did not choose are ignored. Nothing is uploaded, sold, shared or used for advertising, because the app cannot reach the internet.

Media folders (optional)

If you add a media folder, you pick it yourself with Android's folder picker. The app then makes a safety copy, inside its private storage, of each new file that arrives in that folder, so the file is still there if the original is deleted. Files that were in the folder before you added it are not copied. You can remove a folder in the app at any time.

Alerts and background work

The app can show its own notifications, for example when a message was deleted or when the phone has paused the app. You can hide the words in these alerts or switch them off. The app starts its check again after the phone restarts, so that it keeps working; this needs no personal data.

Passcode

If you set a passcode, only a salted, one-way hash of it is stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

Storage, how long things are kept, and deletion

  • Kept messages and media copies are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read them.
  • They are excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer.
  • You decide how long they are kept in the app's settings ("Keep messages for", "Keep media for").
  • You can erase a single message, a whole chat, a media file, or everything, at any time in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept.

When something leaves the app

Only when you ask for it: if you tap Share, Export, Open or "Save a copy", the item you chose is handed to the app or the place you pick. Messages Recovery itself sends nothing.

Messages from other people

The messages you receive were written by other people. The app keeps them for your own personal use on your own phone. Please respect the privacy of the people you talk to and the laws of your country when you use or share what was kept.

Children

Messages Recovery is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Other apps

Messages Recovery is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any messaging app or its owner. It does not connect to those apps or their servers; it only reads the notifications your phone shows you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue, or use the developer contact shown on the app's Google Play page.
